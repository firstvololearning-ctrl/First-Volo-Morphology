Quick Start Guide

Four ways to use First Volo Morphology

Use First Volo for flexible student digital practice, learner-specific teacher-guided instruction, printable and hands-on learning, educator reference tools, or a combination of these resources.

← Back to First Volo

Learn: explore, try, sort and explain

  1. Meet the word part. Look at the picture, say what idea it shows or a word that might connect, then open Reveal the meaning. Use Hear it to listen. This is an unscored introduction.
  2. See it in a word. Open an example to connect the word part with a sentence and check its meaning.
  3. Try it. After the example check, try the next sentence or word where available.
  4. Sort and explain. Sort words using their meanings and contexts. For groups with at least two words and distinct meanings, explain “They all have to do with…” or choose a meaning. You can skip an explanation.
  5. Try another word. After explaining the groups, an additional eligible word with authored context appears when available. Otherwise choose another round.

This is a suggested sequence, not a locked path or developmental scale. Student and educator shared-screen practice use the same Learn activities. Educators can invite oral answers and discuss the comparison. “I explained it aloud” opens a model; it does not record audio or score the explanation. Sort explanations do not create a mastery rating or reward.

Flight, vocabulary and protected-assessment filters continue to apply. WVI and word-list evidence support word selection but do not assign student grades.

From assessment to a practice plan

After reviewing a completed assessment, choose Choose practice from these results. Select the shared learner, Flight, word parts, activity and word challenge. Review these choices and select Save learner’s practice plan. A report’s linked browser learner is preselected only when it matches an eligible shared learner; otherwise choose the learner explicitly.

The plan is an educator decision. Scores do not choose an activity or establish mastery. Use the learner’s responses, support conditions and other evidence. The plan stores practice choices only; it does not upload assessment responses or the Student Code. Saving requires current learner permission, class access and educator ownership. A save is complete only after the confirmation appears.

In the main educator workspace, use Set a practice plan to review, change or clear it. Open practice with this learner opens the saved choices for shared-screen teaching. Adjusting practice choices during a session does not change the saved plan.

In the student workspace, a saved plan appears as Start today’s practice. The learner can use Change practice to access other activities; this does not edit the educator’s plan. A plan remains until the educator changes or clears it. It is not automatically updated each day or after a quiz. The student sees an updated plan when opening or returning to Morphology with an internet connection. This is a starting plan, not a locked sequence or a single-word-part filter.

When you open Morphology

Educator: Choose Practice with a learner, Learners & progress, or Educator resources & guide. Start or review an assessment opens Flight A, B or C assessment tools. Assessment results do not automatically assign practice. Review the item results with other evidence, then choose the Flight, word parts and activity yourself.

Student: Use the Flight and word parts your teacher chose, then choose an activity. Ask your teacher if you are unsure. Standard Words include Familiar and Academic words; Stretch is optional. Activities stay flexible; a tried check is not a mastery score.

Returning: This browser remembers the most recent practice choices separately for each signed-in learner or educator context. Continue opens that activity; it does not resume the exact unanswered question. You can change the choices at any time. These settings are not an assignment and do not sync across devices.

Next Session Guide: In Learners & progress, suggestions use the learner’s latest practice response, not the initial assessment. Review and adjust them using educator judgment.

Assessment records: Formal assessment records are saved in this browser for the educator. Practice cloud sync does not include these assessment records. Keep needed reports separately using the assessment controls.

Word-Part Hoops

Use the Open games button beside practice choices, then choose Shoot hoops beside an unlocked reward. Match the meaning to a word-part ball, adjust aim, then shoot. Line up shot offers aiming help. Harder shots is optional; there is no timer. A correct word part with a missed shot can be retried. Game points do not change learning progress or token unlocks.

The educator start page separates learner selection, word-part choices, and practice activities. Flight and Stretch Words are under Adjust practice. Student practice progress appears only in student mode; educators use Learners & progress for records.

A simpler start

In educator mode, open Practice with a learner to choose a learner. Learners & progress opens records and management. Begin with one word-part type; Combine word parts, Adjust practice, and Choose another activity expand when needed. Use Open Learn (or the current activity name) to jump into practice.

Educators can use Preview game and Preview hoops without earning learner tokens. Previewing does not change learner progress or unlock student rewards. Students see their earned games.

Choose the next step

Educators first choose Set up learning to create or review a saved practice plan, or Practice with a learner to open session choices. Learners & progress opens records separately. Practice settings and activities appear after entering the practice path. Next-step guidance names the current learner when selected and the chosen activity, word-part focus, and Flight. Back to start returns to the entry choices.
