👤 About the Creator

Hi, I'm Alexis, founder of First Volo Learning.

I'm a certified Speech-Language Pathologist (CCC-SLP) with more than 21 years of experience supporting students in a public school district. Since 2004, I have worked as a school-based SLP, developing practical, research-informed resources that support language, literacy, and communication.

At First Volo Learning, I create thoughtfully designed educational resources that make language learning visible, teachable, and achievable. My speech-language therapy materials use meaningful visual supports, explicit instruction, and carefully sequenced practice to help students understand language patterns and use increasingly complex language independently.

I also create interactive Italian language resources built on the same instructional philosophy. Rather than focusing only on vocabulary memorization, these materials help learners progress from recognizing individual words to building sentences and participating in meaningful communication.

Credentials

  • CCC-SLP, American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA)
  • Licensed Speech-Language Pathologist, Massachusetts
  • Licensed Educator: Speech, Language & Hearing Disorders (All Levels)
  • Licensed Educator: English as a Second Language (Grades 5–12)

Whether you are supporting language development in English or learning Italian, I hope First Volo resources help make language learning visible, teachable, and achievable.
