Teacher prompts and example

Replace the sample words with words from today’s lesson. Invite the learner to name each group, explain a connection, and set aside a word if needed. For a hands-on sort, write the words on movable cards before recording the groups.

Group rewrite and replay by re- (“again”), and writing and playing by -ing. Then pair rewrite with writing by the base write, and replay with playing by the base play.
