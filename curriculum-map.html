What Does First Volo Teach?

First Volo teaches students to use meaningful prefixes, roots, and suffixes as tools for understanding and using words. Instruction moves from explicit word-part knowledge and recognition toward structural analysis, meaning inference, word building, and application in context.

What Students Learn

🔎 Recognize Word Parts Identify meaningful prefixes, roots, and suffixes inside increasingly complex words.
💡 Understand Meanings Connect word parts with their meanings and recognize related forms within morpheme families.
🧩 Analyze Word Structure Break words into meaningful parts and explain how those parts contribute to the whole word.
🔍 Infer Word Meaning Use known morphemes as clues to figure out unfamiliar words, with scaffolding when word structure is less transparent.
🏗️ Build & Apply Words Combine meaningful word parts and apply morphologically complex words in sentence context.
🔄 Notice Word Changes Explore how changing a suffix can change a word's form, meaning, or function.

Learning is cumulative. Word parts introduced in earlier Flights continue to appear in later and more complex vocabulary so students can apply previously learned morphology with increasing independence.
