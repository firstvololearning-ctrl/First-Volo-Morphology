What Does First Volo Teach?

First Volo introduces meaningful prefixes, roots, and suffixes across three instructional bands and then revisits those word parts in increasingly varied and complex vocabulary.

About the grade bands: A word-part introduction band shows when a morpheme is introduced. A practice-word band shows the level at which an individual word is currently used. Previously learned word parts continue to appear in later and more complex words.
