# Learn depth expansion — September 28, 2026

Adds eight worked lessons: submarine plus second examples for sub, ion, ment, circum, spect, mit and biblio. Total: 48 two-step lessons across 40 families; 36 sorting rounds remain available. All 96 families have at least two eligible examples in their own Standard flight.

Each of the seven priority families now includes two models, an explicit meaning comparison and supported application to a different word. This application is instructional practice, not an independent mastery score. If the second word is ineligible, the original same-word question remains available; filtered contrasts and secondary navigation disappear as well. Audio follows the visible content.
