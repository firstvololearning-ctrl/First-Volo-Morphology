How to Use the Assessments

1. Pre · Form A: administer before the instructional period. 2. Teach and practice: use First Volo activities. 3. Post · Form B: administer after instruction. Use the same Student Code so the report can compare Pre and Post performance.

Digital or Paper

Open Migration Route 1, Migration Route 2, or Migration Route 3 and choose the form. Use Ready for Takeoff for digital administration, Print Student Form for paper administration, or Enter Paper Results to add a completed paper assessment to the same Pre/Post comparison system.

What the Pre/Post can show

Each form samples word-part knowledge, word analysis, meaning in context, and transfer. Most items ask learners to choose an answer. Two written items ask them to explain relationships using sentence frames. Review the kinds of items the learner found difficult and score the written explanations with the provided rubric.

When to add a brief follow-up
  • To check oral word-part knowledge. Present a fresh item aloud if word reading or writing may be getting in the way. Record the oral response separately.
  • To check word-form production. Give a base and an original sentence with a blank. Ask the learner to say or write the form that fits without answer choices.
  • To find out what help works. After an independent attempt on a fresh item, try one cue and record the supported response separately.

Keep the Pre and Post response conditions comparable and note any accommodations. Use these follow-up observations alongside the informal scores. Nelson-Strouts & Bridges (2024) on task and administration choices.

Results are intended for informal instructional progress monitoring and should be interpreted alongside other classroom, intervention, and assessment information.
