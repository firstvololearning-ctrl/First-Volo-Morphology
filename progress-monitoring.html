Longer-Term Morphology Progress Monitoring Probes

Use these periodic probes to monitor broader morphology skills over time. Choose the grade band that best matches the student's instructional level.

Grades 2–3

Beginning Morphology

Monitor developing knowledge of meaningful word parts, word analysis, word building, and meaning inference.

📄 Open Grades 2–3 Probe
Grades 4–5

Intermediate Morphology

Monitor use of word parts for structural analysis, word construction, transfer, and unfamiliar-word meaning.

📄 Open Grades 4–5 Probe
Grades 6–8

Advanced Morphology

Monitor morphological analysis, academic-word inference, word construction, and explicit reasoning about word structure.

📄 Open Grades 6–8 Probe

Using the Probes

Forms A and B may be alternated across administrations. Results are intended for informal instructional progress monitoring and should be interpreted alongside other classroom, intervention, and assessment information.
