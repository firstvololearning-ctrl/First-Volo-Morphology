First Volo Morphology

Prefixes, Roots, and Word Building

First Volo Morphology provides explicit, scaffolded instruction in meaningful word parts. Students move from learning and recognizing morphemes to using them to infer word meanings and build new words.

Illustrated cards support initial learning and feedback. Text-only word-part tiles are used during questions so that the pictures do not reveal the correct answer.

Related forms such as in-, im-, il-, and ir- are taught together as a meaning family and practiced separately when students identify or build actual words.
