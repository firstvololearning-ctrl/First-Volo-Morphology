FIRST VOLO MORPHOLOGY

Build, move, analyze, and explain.

These materials are designed for hands-on small-group, intervention, classroom, and individual practice. Students manipulate meaningful word parts, construct related words, record word sums and meanings, and use word-level and sentence clues to guide their thinking.

A Developing Printable Series

Printable materials follow the same developmental progression as First Volo Morphology. Earlier practice emphasizes familiar bases and high-frequency affixes. Later printable sets increasingly explore high-utility roots, related word families, layered word construction, and meaning in context.

Additional printable sets will be added as the instructional library grows.
