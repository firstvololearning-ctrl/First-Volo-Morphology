FIRST VOLO MORPHOLOGY

Build, move, analyze, and explain.

These materials are designed for hands-on small-group, intervention, classroom, and individual practice. Students manipulate meaningful word parts, construct related words, record word sums and meanings, and use word-level and sentence clues to guide their thinking.

Sort and Explain

Group the same words in two ways and explain each connection. Choose a sample for any Route or enter words from the set you’re teaching. Use the worksheet on screen or print it for teacher-led practice.

Anchor Word Review

Use after teaching the parts shown on an unlocked Anchor Word card. Recall a part, explain what it adds, and try it in a different word.

TEACH ACROSS ROUTES

Route teaching packs

Each pack includes a display-ready anchor chart, a student practice page, an answer key, and the route’s full word-part list with example sentences. Print page 1 for the chart or page 2 for student copies.

Use the chart to introduce the focus. Use the practice page after teaching its listed parts. For the other printables, start with the parts learners know and add cards as you teach them.

  • Route 1 · What did the prefix change? (PDF)
    Use the practice page after teaching re- (Set 1 · Core Word Parts) and pre- and mis- (Set 2 · Common Meaning Changes).
  • Route 2 · Find the idea that connects the words (PDF)
    Use the practice page after teaching geo (Roots 2) and struct (Roots 3).
  • Route 3 · Use the root, then check the sentence (PDF)
    Use the practice page after teaching pel (Roots 2). Review cred and aud from the same set if learners need help with the comparison words.

Build & Discover across routes

Each packet includes cut-apart cards, build mats, word and sentence clues, and a recording sheet. Print the mats in landscape at actual size.

  • Route 1 · PLAY (PDF)
    Use after teaching re-, -ed, -ing, and verb -s in Set 1 · Core Word Parts.
  • Route 2 · DICT (PDF)
    Use after teaching dict in Roots 1. Review pre- from Route 1 Set 2, the verb endings from Set 1, and -ion from Set 4.
  • Route 3 · JECT (PDF)
    Use after teaching ject in Roots 1. Review pro- from Route 2 Prefixes 2, the Route 1 Set 1 verb endings, and -ion from Route 1 Set 4.

A Developing Printable Series

Available printable materials support selected parts of First Volo Morphology. Earlier materials emphasize familiar bases and high-frequency affixes. Later printable sets increasingly explore high-utility roots, related word families, layered word construction, and meaning in context. The current printable library does not yet represent every digital activity, word part, or learning set.

Additional printable sets will be added as the instructional library grows.
