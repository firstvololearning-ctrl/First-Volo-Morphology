Check progress when teaching a set
  1. Plan the set. Open Scope and Sequence and identify the parts in the learner’s Migration Map set. Teach them over as many sessions as the learner needs.
  2. Check after teaching. Ask the learner to identify or explain the taught part, then apply it in a fresh word. Use a task that fits the part, such as explaining a meaning or producing a form that fits a sentence. Listen to the first attempt before giving a cue.
  3. Check again on a later date. Ask the learner to recall the part and use a different practice word. Use the set progress in Learners & Progress to decide which parts and skills to revisit.

Save eligible practice with Record an observed response in Learners & Progress. The app uses those responses toward the learner’s set token.
